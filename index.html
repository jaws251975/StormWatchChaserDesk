WIND PROFILE

Upper-air analysis

Wind measurements at different pressure levels help describe how winds change with altitude.

850 hPa: Lower-atmosphere winds.

500 hPa: Mid-atmosphere winds.

Bulk shear: Calculated from the vector difference between 10-meter and 500-hPa winds. It is an approximate layer measurement, not a formal sounding analysis.

ADVANCED PRODUCTS

Additional storm parameters

SRH: Not currently supplied by this weather-model data connection.

STP: Not currently calculated. It requires several atmospheric ingredients.

Mesocyclone detection: Requires specialized radar products.

These products will be added when we connect suitable observational data sources.

Important weather information

ChaseDesk is an educational and remote meteorological analysis project, not an emergency warning service. Model estimates are not direct observations and radar mosaics may be delayed or incomplete. Never use this dashboard to make safety-critical decisions or to approach severe weather. Follow official National Weather Service warnings and local emergency guidance.
