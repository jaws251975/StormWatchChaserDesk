850 hPa: Lower-atmosphere winds.

500 hPa: Mid-atmosphere winds.

Bulk shear: Approximate vector difference between 10-meter and 500-hPa winds. It is not a formal sounding analysis.

Important weather information

ChaseDesk is an educational and remote meteorological analysis project, not an emergency warning service. Model estimates are not direct observations. Never use this dashboard or its simulations to make safety-critical decisions or approach severe weather. Follow official National Weather Service warnings and local emergency guidance.
